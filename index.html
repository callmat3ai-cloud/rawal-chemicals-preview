Operational Standards

Safety & Quality Commitment

Handling and transporting bulk chemicals demands absolute adherence to safety codes and stringent quality verification. Our operational processes guarantee compliance at every step.

Rigorous Quality Checks

Over 45 Years in Business

Rawal Acids Private Limited

Established in 1975, Rawal Acids Private Limited (known in the trade as Rawal Chemicals) was founded with a vision to supply vital industrial chemicals to the growing manufacturing landscape of North India. Over the decades, we have evolved from a local acid distributor into one of the region's largest chemical wholesalers.

We pride ourselves on our core competence in Hydrochloric Acid, which forms the backbone of our operations. Delivering over 4,000 MT of chemical material monthly, our organization serves small enterprises and multinational corporations alike, with safety and quality embedded at the core of our business.
